diymore ESP32 + 1.9" LCD, seen from above with the screen facing you, USB-C on the left and the antenna on the right. Pins are numbered T1–T15 along the top and B1–B15 along the bottom, counting from the USB end.

Check the orientation before wiring. Plug in USB only, then meter the two pins nearest the USB socket against GND. Top-left (T1, VIN) should read about 4.7–5V and bottom-left (B1) should read 3.3V. If they're the other way round, the board is upside down compared with this drawing.
