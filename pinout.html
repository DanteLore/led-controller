LED controller pinout: diymore LCD board

Seen from above with the screen facing you and USB-C on the left. The pin names are printed on the underside; the rows here are confirmed on the real board (2026-11-19). Pins are numbered T1–T15 along the top edge and B1–B15 along the bottom edge, counting from the USB end.

Check the orientation before wiring. Turn the board over like a page, keeping USB on the left, and compare the labels with the underside view further down: VIN should be at the near edge and 3.3V at the far edge. Metered on the board 2026-11-19: T1 = VIN (top left), B1 = 3.3V (bottom left).
