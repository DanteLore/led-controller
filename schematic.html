Rules

  • All grounds are one net: PSU −, C1, strip, U2 in and out, U1 and every peripheral. Run the strip's ground straight back to the PSU so strip current doesn't flow through U1's ground.
  • The strip's 24V pad never goes near U1. A slip puts 24V on a GPIO and kills the ESP32.
  • Power U1 from USB or U2, never both. Unplug U2's output before plugging in USB to flash.
  • Knobs and mic take 3.3V (B1), not VIN.
  • No external pull-ups are needed: the firmware turns on the internal pull-ups for the knobs and the switch.
  • Leave GPIO2, 4, 15, 18, 23 and 32 alone (onboard LCD) and GPIO12 unconnected (boot pin). Spare: GPIO5 (boot pin), 34, 36, 39 (input only).
